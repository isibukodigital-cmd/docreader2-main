Service and accounts

Doc Reader provides a document question-and-answer service. Access is tied to a personal or organization workspace. Organization access may require verification and platform approval. Workspace administrators are responsible for managing membership and document access groups.

Customer content and permissions

Customers retain their rights in documents and other content they submit. Customers must have the rights and authority required to upload and process that content, including permission to share it with configured subprocessors where hosted models are enabled. Do not upload information that the customer is not authorized to process.

AI-generated responses

Responses may be incomplete, incorrect or out of date. Users must check the cited source and independently verify important or consequential information. The service is not professional, legal, medical or financial advice.

Acceptable use and security

Customers must not bypass access controls, interfere with the service, or use it unlawfully. Customers should promptly notify the service operator of suspected unauthorized access. The final agreement must define support, incident-notification, service availability and customer security responsibilities.

Termination rights, suspension rules, warranties, liability limits, dispute resolution and any mandatory statutory rights require review and completion by legal counsel. No liability cap or warranty exclusion is specified by this draft.

South Africa is the intended governing jurisdiction. The final governing-law, venue and dispute-resolution wording must be reviewed by South African counsel before these terms are accepted.
